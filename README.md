# 🏮 Chronicles of the Mandarin Academy (中文学院奇遇记)
**Open-Source Interactive 2D RPG for Mandarin Chinese Learning (Lessons 1–3)**

Built as a Project-Based Learning (PBL) initiative for our Mandarin Chinese course at SRM Institute of Science and Technology (Kattankulathur).

## ✨ Key Features
- **3-Chapter Syllabus-Aligned Storyline:** Covers all 120 vocabulary items and 16 grammar patterns across Lessons 1–3 of *A Course in Contemporary Chinese* (Book 1).
- **2-Pass Continuous Calligraphy Notebook (`HanziWriter`):** Players write complete multi-character answers continuously from left to right—first with stroke outlines & animations ON, and second from memory with outlines & animations OFF.
- **Interactive Codex (`典籍`):** Searchable dictionary (by Hanzi, Pinyin, or English) with live stroke-order animation popups, Mandarin audio playback, and Grammar Pattern scrolls.
- **Click-to-Lookup & Unlimited Audio (`🔊`):** Click any Chinese word in dialogues or quizzes to jump straight to its Codex entry, or replay native Mandarin pronunciation anytime.
- **Zero-Backend Checkpoint Saves:** Automatic progress saving via browser `localStorage`.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally
1. Click the green **`<> Code`** button above and select **`Download ZIP`**.
2. Extract the ZIP folder completely on your laptop.
3. Double-click **`index.html`** in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (requires an internet connection for CDN fonts and stroke data).
